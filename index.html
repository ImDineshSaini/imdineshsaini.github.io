About

I am a Software Architect with 12+ years of hands-on experience designing, developing, and delivering enterprise-grade solutions. Over the last 2+ years, I have transitioned into architectural leadership — building scalable, cloud-native, and AI-augmented systems for the global banking industry.

I am passionate about responsible AI adoption, scalable architecture, and building systems that deliver tangible business impact.
